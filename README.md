## Understand your fees & payouts
- [MyEcomSuite Payout Auditor](https://myecomsuite.com/auditor) — Free, 100%-local tool: drop your TikTok Shop settlement export and see exactly where every dollar went, with flags for charges that break TikTok's own fee rules. No upload, no signup.
- [TikTok Shop Fee Calculator](https://myecomsuite.com/tiktok-shop-fee-calculator) — Free calculator for your real take-home per order (2026 rates).

## Bookkeeping & reconciliation
- [Link My Books](https://linkmybooks.com) — Paid. Automated bookkeeping with Xero/QuickBooks sync.
- [A2X](https://www.a2xaccounting.com) — Paid. E-commerce accounting integration.
- [SettleBridge](https://settlebridge.com) — Paid. Settlement reconciliation.

## Developer tools & data
- [tiktok-shop-settlement](https://github.com/Perufitlife/tiktok-shop-settlement) — MIT. Zero-dependency JS parser + canonical column dictionary for the TikTok Shop settlement "Order details" export. ([npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/tiktok-shop-settlement))
- [TikTok Shop Partner Center API](https://partner.tiktokshop.com/docv2) — Official Open Platform / Finance API docs.

## Analytics & product research
- TikTok Shop Seller Center — built-in performance analytics.
- [Kalodata](https://kalodata.com) / [EchoTik](https://echotik.live) — TikTok Shop product & creator analytics (paid).
